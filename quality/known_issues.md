# Known issues (documented, not hidden)

Values are kept as recorded unless stated otherwise. Readings that are present but should not be read as measurements
carry a flag in `quality/cell_flags.csv` (one row per flagged cell: `system`, `ts_local`, `variable`, `flag`); per-day
counts are in `quality/daily_qc.csv` and per-group usable coverage in `quality/daily_coverage.csv`.

| # | Issue | Period | Effect on the data | Handling in this package |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Readings marked invalid by the SCADA were copied into the processed monthly workbooks** (`Data_YYYY_MM.xlsx`), which are the source of the 2023–2024 values. When the SCADA loses a signal it repeats the last value with `Validity = 0`. | 2023–2024 | repeated values that are not measurements (e.g. 2023-03-20 to 2023-04-12, when every variable stays constant) | flag `scada_invalid`, derived from the WinCC exports in `raw/`; `validity_checked` in `daily_qc.csv` says whether a WinCC export covers the day |
| 2 | **Frozen recordings**: the whole system repeats its values for hours | 2023-09-14, 2025-09-01/02 and others | constant series | flag `frozen` (most variables unchanged for ≥ 1 h) |
| 3 | **Probes not connected read exactly 0.0 °C** (panel surface probes until late September 2023, in July 2024 and on unit B from October to December 2025; cold tank on some days) | 2023–2025 | zeros that are not temperatures | flag `zero` (0.0 °C for ≥ 1 h) |
| 4 | **Solarimeter faults**: stuck readings (256, 511, 698 W/m² repeated for hours, also at night) and whole days at 0 W/m² (autumn 2022, November–December 2025 for unit A). The sensor did deliver valid data in most of 2022–2025. | 2022–2025 | stuck or zero irradiance | flags `stuck` and `zero` |
| 5 | **Infrared radiation recorded differently by period**: 2019 `ir_net` in W/m² (negative = net loss to the sky) but, until 2019-07-24 ~13:00, the channel gave positive values (+360 to +440 W/m², probably incoming longwave); 2022–2024 only a raw signal (`ir_raw_signal`, undocumented scale that changes between months, negative values in 2024); 2024–2025 `E_FIR neto` → `ir_net_w_m2`; in 2025 the unit-B pyrgeometer tags stay at 33 W/m²: there is a single pyrgeometer, on unit A | all | the series are not directly comparable | flag `definition_differs` (2019) and `stuck`; the overflow code 996298.6 of the raw signal is removed (NaN); sign and unit kept as recorded (no unified convention) |
| 6 | **2019, 10–25 July: logger header shifted by one column** (files `RCE.CSV` 00–08 have an empty column in the header, the data follow the normal order) | 2019-07-10 to 07-25 | in v0.1 five variables were swapped (hot tank ↔ collector flow, RCE flow ↔ a temperature) | **fixed in v0.2**: columns mapped by position; the second RCE temperature of the logger (`RC_IN_TEMP`, the RCE outlet, `t_circuit_4_c` from v0.2.3) is recovered |
| 7 | **Logger placeholder `1e-30`** written when no reading was available | 2019-07-10 to 07-15 (all of 2019-07-13) | in v0.1 it was averaged with real readings (diluted values) | **fixed in v0.2**: removed before averaging; 2019-07-13 has no data |
| 8 | **Units in 2025.** Until 2024 there was a single prototype (`system = RCE`). In 2025 it became unit A, automated and driven by the controller, and unit B was built next to it as the manual test bench. Tags with suffix `_RCEa` / `_RCEb` belong to units A and B; unsuffixed 2025 tags (cold-tank stratification probes, auxiliary tank probes, pressure P21) are reported as `system = RCE`. Unit B has no pyrgeometer, cold-tank, outdoor-air or solar values of its own: the site values recorded with unit A apply to both units. | 2025 | in v0.1 unit A appeared as `RCE` and unit B was missing in November–December | **fixed in v0.2** |
| 9 | **Relative humidity in 2025** reads about 13 points lower than the reference station Meteocat YJ (Lleida), while the bias is within ±3 points in 2019–2024; air temperature is 1.1–1.3 °C warmer than the reference in every year | 2025 (RH); all (T) | biased humidity | not corrected; documented |
| 10 | **Heat-transfer fluid is water-glycol 30 %**, not water | all | energy computed with water properties is ~10 % too high | documented; no energy metrics are shipped |
| 11 | **Discontinuous coverage.** Several periods contain only radiometer data (autumn 2022 to January 2023, November 2024 to August 2025); 2020 is in `raw/` only; months are partial | — | partial days | `daily_coverage.csv` (per group) and the month table in `README.md` |
| 12 | **Timestamps are local time** (Europe/Madrid) without an explicit zone in the sources | all | on 2023-03-26 the hour 02:00–03:00 does not exist; on 2023-10-29 it occurs twice and only the first pass is kept | `ts_local` + derived `ts_utc`; affected intervals have `ts_utc` missing (`ts_utc_nat_n`) |
| 13 | **No actuator states**: valve positions (EV1/EV2), cover position, pump and heater commands and controller decisions are not in the exports; in 2025 electrical meters (`historico_energia_A/B`, `EnergymeterRCE_A/B`, `TaulaKW0`) record pump and heater power; their units are not documented | all | the physical mode (which tank is charged, cover open or closed) cannot be reconstructed; `flow_period` only says when water circulated | meters available in `raw/` only |
| 14 | **Days the laboratory diary declares unreliable** (water circulation problems 2023-07-09…13; experiments with valves 2023-10-23; "the system was not working properly until 2024-02-05", start of the period not stated) | 2023–2024 | measurements of a system not working as intended | `diary_flag = unreliable` in `labels/days.csv`; periods in `quality/known_events.csv` |
| 15 | **Sampling.** WinCC logs cyclically every 300 s (not on change); the processed workbooks copy those records; the 2019 logger recorded every minute. Overlapping exports repeat records (a few hundred differ in value). | all | — | one value per variable and 5-minute interval; precedence: processed workbook of the day's month > other workbook > 2019 logger > WinCC exports (first by file path) |
| 16 | **Probe T7** shows impossible values (up to 842 °C) on some 2024 days | 2024 | — | counted in `out_of_range_n`; value kept |
| 17 | **QC reference ranges tolerate sensor zero-offsets** (solar down to −20 W/m² at night, RH up to 101 %, wind and pressure slightly below 0) | all | — | see `docs/variable_map.yaml` |
| 18 | **Not every file in `raw/` is a native instrument export**: `kind` in `raw/index.csv` distinguishes `instrument_export`, `processed_workbook`, `spreadsheet_copy`, `hand_edited` (2019 CSVs with columns typed in by hand) and `logger_binary` (`.HDT`); `used_in_harmonized` says whether a file contributed values | — | — | `quality/lineage.csv` lists the files behind each day |
| 19 | **Physical location of circuit probes T1–T11 and stratification probes** not documented; T2 and T4 are the RCE inlet and outlet, their exact position is not documented | all | variables documented as `pending` | `docs/data_dictionary.csv`, `docs/glossary.md` |
| 20 | **2019 RCE inlet and outlet named from the cold-tank side**: logger tag `RC_OU_TEM` is the RCE inlet and `RC_IN_TEMP` the RCE outlet. With flow at night the inlet is warmer than the outlet in 96 % of the 2019 intervals, as expected in radiative cooling | 2019 | up to v0.2.2 the outlet was published under an inlet name (see `CHANGELOG.md`, 0.2.3); from 2019-07-11 09:15 to 2019-07-12 11:10 a hand-edited source file repeats the inlet in the outlet column | **fixed in v0.2.3**: inlet in `t_circuit_2_c`, outlet in `t_circuit_4_c` (as T2 and T4 in 2023–2025); the repeated cells carry the flag `definition_differs` |
